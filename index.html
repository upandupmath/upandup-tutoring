About Your Tutor

Meet Brother Truth

Founder, Up And Up Educational Services

Brother Truth is a veteran mathematics educator with more than two decades of classroom experience across Washington, D.C. and Maryland public schools. He currently teaches Geometry and Algebra I full-time at Ron Brown College Preparatory High School — the only all-male public high school in the District — and was selected for EmpowerK12's inaugural BrightSpot Fellowship — a cohort of just eight teachers across the District recognized for measurable student achievement. Of those eight, four were chosen from D.C. Public Charter Schools and four from D.C. Public Schools. Brother Truth was one of only two high school teachers selected.

A graduate of Morgan State University, he has taught in D.C. Public Schools, public charter schools, and Prince George's County Public Schools — reaching students at every level of confidence, from the ones who love math to the ones who dread it. Beyond the classroom, he has spent years running youth enrichment programs across the region, building a teaching style that meets students where they are and moves them forward.

Up And Up Educational Services brings that same classroom-tested instruction into focused, one-on-one virtual sessions for students in grades 6 through 10.

Good to Know

Policies

The 4-session minimum

Every sign-up commits to 4 sessions, billed in two payments — sessions 1 & 2 at booking, sessions 3 & 4 auto-charged before session 3. This protects the calendar time reserved for your child and gives enough runway to actually make progress on a topic — even if it started as "just one section of homework."

Rescheduling

Life happens. Reschedule any session with at least 24 hours' notice through the calendar link in your confirmation email, at no charge.

Missed sessions

Sessions missed without 24 hours' notice are counted toward your 4-session minimum and are not refunded, since that time was held exclusively for your child.

Virtual only

All sessions take place over Google Meet. A unique meeting link is generated and emailed to both the parent and the student (if a student email is provided) ahead of every booked session.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

What if my child only needs help with one topic?

That's the most common reason families sign up. The 4-session minimum gives enough time to actually close the gap on one unit or skill, rather than a single session that only scratches the surface.

What grade levels do you cover?

Grades 6 through 10 — from middle school math and Pre-Algebra through Algebra I and Geometry.

How does payment work?

Your 4-session package is split into two payments: sessions 1 and 2 are paid when you book, and sessions 3 and 4 are automatically charged to the same card a few days before session 3. All invoicing is handled through PayPal, and we accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) — no PayPal account required.

How do we join the Google Meet?

A unique Google Meet link is generated for each session and emailed to both the parent and the student (if a student email is provided) in advance, tied to the time you book on the calendar.

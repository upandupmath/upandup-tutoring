Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Last updated September 2, 2026

Up And Up Educational Services ("we," "us," "our") provides virtual math tutoring for students in grades 6 through 10. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the rights you have as a parent or guardian.

We take children's privacy seriously. Because some of our students may be under 13, we follow the principles of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for all student data, regardless of the student's age.

1. Information We Collect

From parents/guardians at booking:

  • Parent or guardian name and email address
  • Student's grade level and subject area
  • Student email address (optional — only if provided by the parent)
  • Session scheduling preferences
  • Notes about what the student needs help with

From the placement test (optional, with consent):

  • Parent name and email, student name
  • Student email (optional)
  • Grade level, assessment score, and area breakdown
  • Timestamp of parental consent

Automatically during use:

  • IP address (for rate limiting only — not stored long-term)
  • Payment transaction records through PayPal (we do not store credit card numbers)

2. How We Use This Information

  • To schedule and deliver tutoring sessions
  • To create Google Calendar events and Google Meet links
  • To send booking confirmations, invoices, and session reminders
  • To process payments through PayPal
  • To assess skill level and recommend appropriate instruction

We do not use student information for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to the tutoring service.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Social Security numbers, government IDs, or financial account numbers
  • Cookies for tracking or advertising
  • Location data
  • We do not allow students to make their information publicly available
  • We do not condition participation on providing more information than is reasonably necessary

4. Parental Consent

All bookings are made by a parent or guardian, not the student. By completing a booking or submitting a placement test, you are providing consent for us to collect and use the information described in this policy for the purpose of delivering tutoring services to your child.

The placement test includes a consent checkbox that records the date and time of your agreement. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (see Section 8).

5. How We Share Information

We share information only with the services necessary to deliver tutoring:

  • PayPal — to process payments
  • Google Workspace — to create Calendar events and Meet links
  • Resend — to deliver transactional emails
  • Supabase — to securely store booking and assessment data (SOC 2 Type II certified)

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with any other third parties. We do not share information for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
  • Database access is restricted by row-level security policies
  • API endpoints are rate-limited to prevent abuse
  • Payment processing is handled entirely by PayPal — we never see or store card numbers

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain booking and assessment records for the duration of the tutoring relationship plus one school year. After that, records are deleted. You may request deletion of your child's data at any time — we will remove all personally identifiable information within 30 days.

8. Your Rights as a Parent or Guardian

You have the right to:

  • Review the personal information we have collected about your child
  • Request deletion of your child's personal information
  • Refuse further collection of your child's information
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us below. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date above and notify current families by email. We will obtain new parental consent before collecting or using information in any way not covered by this policy.
